Happy 50th Anniversary to Ogun State, the Gateway State.


We celebrate five decades of resilience, growth, and remarkable contributions to Nigeria’s development.
Ogun State has remained a land of industry, learning, culture, and innovation, truly living up to its identity as a gateway of opportunity and progress.


As the state marks this golden milestone, we celebrate and honour every stakeholder, past and present, whose vision, service, and commitment have contributed to the betterment of the state. 
We also acknowledge the founding fathers, the dedication of leaders across generations, and the hardworking spirit of the people who continue to build and sustain this land.


May the years ahead bring greater development, stronger institutions, and expanded opportunities for all who call Ogun State home.


May the Gateway State continue to open doors to innovation, economic growth, cultural excellence, and national advancement.


Congratulations to Ogun State at 50.Glory to God!
